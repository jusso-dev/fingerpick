# FingerPick privacy policy

_Last updated: 5 November 2026_

FingerPick doesn't collect, store, share or sell any personal information.

- **No data collection.** The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting, and it makes no network requests.
- **On your device only.** Your settings (mode, countdown, minimum fingers, haptics) are saved on your iPhone with standard iOS storage and never leave it. Deleting the app deletes them.
- **Touches.** Finger positions are used only while a round is running, to draw the rings and pick a result. They're never recorded.
- **Children.** FingerPick is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/jusso-dev/fingerpick/issues>.
